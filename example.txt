1. INTRODUCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
This Statement of Work describes the deliverables-based services that
Brightwave Technology Solutions, LLC (the "Vendor") will provide to the
Lone Star Regional Water Authority (the "Customer") for the Permit
Management Portal Modernization project.

At a summary level, the Vendor will replace the Customer's legacy,
paper-assisted water-use permitting process with a secure, web-based
portal. The portal will allow applicants to submit permit applications and
supporting documents online, pay fees electronically, and track status,
and will allow Customer staff to review, approve and report on permits.
The resulting deliverables include project and requirements documentation,
a system design, a two-release configured portal, migrated historical
data, test and training materials, and go-live support.


2. BACKGROUND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Lone Star Regional Water Authority is a regional government entity
that manages water supply, conservation and permitting for a 12-county
service area. LSRWA processes approximately 9,500 water-use, well-drilling
and discharge permit applications each year.

The Customer is contracting for this deliverables-based service because
its current permitting system, built in 2008 on an unsupported platform,
relies on paper forms, manual data entry and email. Average permit
turnaround is 41 days, staff spend an estimated 30 percent of their time
on re-keying data, and the system no longer receives vendor security
patches.

In 2024 the LSRWA Board of Directors approved the Permit Modernization
Initiative, funded through the FY2027 capital budget. Phase 1 of the
initiative (this SOW) covers the applicant portal, staff review workflow,
online payments and migration of five years of permit history. A possible
Phase 2 would add a mobile inspection application and GIS integration and
is not part of this SOW.


3. SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 PROJECT-BASED SERVICES

3.1.1 Scope of Work
The Vendor shall perform the following work:
  a. Plan and manage the project in accordance with the project management
     plan.
  b. Analyze current business processes and document business, functional
     and security requirements.
  c. Design the portal architecture, user experience, integrations and
     security controls.
  d. Configure and build the portal in two releases:
       Release 1 - applicant registration, online application intake,
                   document upload and staff review workflow.
       Release 2 - fee calculation and online payment, permit issuance,
                   notifications, dashboards and standard reports.
  e. Migrate five (5) years of historical permit records (approximately
     47,000 records and 120,000 attached documents) from the legacy
     system.
  f. Integrate the portal with the Customer's payment gateway, Active
     Directory single sign-on and email service.
  g. Plan and conduct system, integration, performance, security and user
     acceptance testing.
  h. Develop and deliver training for Customer staff and public user
     guides.
  i. Support cutover to production and provide thirty (30) days of
     post-go-live support ("hypercare").

Out of scope: mobile applications, GIS integration, hardware procurement,
legacy system decommissioning, and migration of records older than five
years.

3.1.2 Risks, Assumptions and Constraints
  Risks:
    - Legacy data may contain duplicates, missing fields or inconsistent
      formats that extend migration effort.
    - Key Customer subject matter experts may have limited availability
      during the annual summer permit peak (June - August).
    - The payment gateway provider may change its API during the project.
  Assumptions:
    - The Customer will provide access to the legacy database, test
      environments and SMEs within two (2) business days of request.
    - The Customer will provide a single decision-maker for requirements
      and design approvals.
    - The portal will be hosted in the Customer's existing approved
      government cloud tenant.
  Constraints:
    - Total fixed price of $439,000.00; no work may begin on additional
      scope without an approved change request.
    - The portal must go live no later than August 31, 2027, before the
      start of the FY2028 permit cycle.
    - The solution must comply with Texas Administrative Code Chapter 202
      and applicable accessibility requirements (WCAG 2.1 AA).

3.1.4 Detailed Description of Deliverables

  Deliverable 1 - Project Management Plan
    Includes scope, work breakdown structure, baseline schedule, resource
    plan, communications plan, risk and issue management plan, change
    control plan and quality plan.
    Format: MS Word and MS Project.

  Deliverable 2 - Requirements and Business Process Analysis Report
    Documents current-state and future-state processes, user stories,
    functional and non-functional requirements, and a requirements
    traceability matrix.
    Format: MS Word and MS Excel.

  Deliverable 3 - System Architecture and Security Design Document
    Describes the technical architecture, data model, integrations, user
    interface wireframes, role-based access model, audit logging and
    security controls.
    Format: MS Word.

  Deliverable 4 - Data Migration Plan
    Defines data mapping, cleansing rules, migration approach, validation
    criteria, rehearsal schedule and rollback procedures.
    Format: MS Word and MS Excel.

  Deliverable 5 - Portal Release 1
    Deployed and configured applicant registration, online application
    intake, document upload and staff review workflow in the Customer's
    test environment, with release notes.
    Format: Deployed system and MS Word release notes.

  Deliverable 6 - Test Plan and User Acceptance Test (UAT) Cases
    Describes test strategy, environments, test cases, defect management
    and entry/exit criteria for all test phases.
    Format: MS Word and MS Excel.

  Deliverable 7 - Data Migration Execution and Validation Report
    Migrated production-ready data with reconciliation of record counts
    and sampled field-level validation results.
    Format: Migrated data and MS Excel / MS Word report.

  Deliverable 8 - Portal Release 2
    Deployed fee calculation, online payment, permit issuance,
    notifications, dashboards and standard reports, with release notes.
    Format: Deployed system and MS Word release notes.

  Deliverable 9 - Training Materials and Delivered Training
    Staff training curriculum, up to six (6) instructor-led sessions for
    up to 40 staff, administrator guide and public user guides.
    Format: MS PowerPoint, MS Word and PDF.

  Deliverable 10 - Go-Live, Hypercare and Project Closeout Report
    Production cutover, 30 days of hypercare support, final lessons
    learned, as-built documentation and knowledge transfer.
    Format: Deployed system and MS Word report.

3.1.5 Acceptance Criteria
A deliverable is accepted when it (a) conforms to its description in
Section 3.1.4, (b) meets the quality and service level requirements in
Section 6, (c) is submitted in an approved format on or before its due
date, and (d) is approved in writing by the Customer Contract Manager.

Additional criteria:
  - Document deliverables: no critical or high-priority review comments
    remain open; the Customer has signed off on content.
  - Software releases: 100 percent of critical and high-priority test
    cases pass; no open Severity 1 or Severity 2 defects; no more than
    ten (10) open Severity 3 defects with agreed fix dates; passes the
    Customer's security scan with no critical or high findings.
  - Data migration: record counts reconcile at 99.5 percent or better and
    sampled field-level accuracy is 99 percent or better, with exceptions
    documented and approved.
  - Training: at least 90 percent of the target staff attend, and the
    Customer approves the materials.

3.1.6 Project Completion Criteria
The project is complete when:
  - All ten deliverables have been accepted in writing;
  - The portal has been in production for thirty (30) days with no open
    Severity 1 or Severity 2 defects;
  - All open issues are resolved or formally transferred to the Customer;
  - Knowledge transfer and as-built documentation are complete; and
  - The Customer Contract Manager issues written project closeout.
